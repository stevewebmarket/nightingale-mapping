# Nightingale Mapping

**Mapping the hidden mathematical structure of sound — starting with the nightingale.**

We are building a system that turns real audio into a clean mathematical "ladder" of ratios, preserves that structure under transformations, and eventually uncovers new mathematics from nature.

### Core Objectives

1. **Structure-Preserving Bidirectional Layer (Foundation)**  
   Real audio → mathematical ratio ladder → regenerated audio that preserves structure under pitch shift, time scaling, and harmonic density.

2. **Reproducibility & Verification (Truth Layer)**  
   Everything must be fully runnable from the public repo with one command. Local repo = single source of truth.

3. **Optimization Engine (Improvement Layer)**  
   A genuine self-improving loop that delivers measurable gains in invariance using real metrics on original audio.

4. **Temporal & Rhythm Layer**  
   Extend the ladder to time: reliable IOI extraction, tempo invariance, and bidirectional rhythm mapping.

5. **Discovery Layer – New Mathematics from Nature**  
   Use the system on nightingale song (and other natural signals) to reveal non-obvious symmetries and structures.

6. **Scaling, Demonstration & Readiness**  
   Create clean demos, document failure modes, and prepare the project for deeper collaboration and resources.

### Current Status (as of April 2026)
- We are primarily working in **Section 1** (Foundation) and **Section 3** (Optimization).
- The self-improvement loop and bidirectional test are operational.
- Next focus: Locking real pitch invariance (M1.2) and improving the optimization metric (M3.1).
